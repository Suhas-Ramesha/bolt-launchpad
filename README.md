# BOLT Energy Gel Website

I built this website as a high-impact product launch experience for **AbiYt Energy Gel** — designed to feel fast, modern, and impossible to ignore.

## What I Built

- A sleek, responsive product website focused on conversion and brand storytelling  
- A bold hero section that introduces AbiYt Energy Gel as the go-to fuel for athletes and high performers  
- Product-focused layouts that highlight benefits, ingredients, usage, and performance outcomes  
- Clear call-to-action sections to drive purchases, sign-ups, and product interest

## Standout Features

- **Lightning-fast performance** for smooth browsing on desktop and mobile  
- **Mobile-first responsive design** so the site looks sharp on every screen size  
- **Clean UI + strong visual hierarchy** to keep attention on the product and key messages  
- **Feature and benefit blocks** that make the value of AbiYt Energy Gel instantly clear  
- **Conversion-oriented sections** including CTA banners, trust messaging, and user-focused content flow

## Build Approach

I approached this like a real product launch:

1. Defined a clear product narrative (energy, endurance, speed, recovery)
2. Structured the page to guide visitors from awareness to action
3. Crafted sections around what customers care about most
4. Optimized layout and responsiveness for a polished cross-device experience
5. Refined content and visual balance to keep the experience premium and focused

## Project Goal

The goal was to create a website that doesn’t just present AbiYt Energy Gel — it **sells the experience** of peak performance.
